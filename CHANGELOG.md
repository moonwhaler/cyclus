## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23

The first release of Cyclus for Android: a private cycle tracker that keeps everything on your device.

### Track and understand
- **Today:** cycle ring with an explanation, your status, today's log, what's coming up and the week at a glance.
- **Make Today yours:** reorder, hide and add cards; large or compact ring. Optional cards: cycle vs. usual, temperature curve, mood & energy, questions for your doctor and your current cycle phase.
- **Quick picks:** up to 8 symptoms, feelings or tags in one tap.
- Log, calendar, insights, your patterns and a 7-day outlook.
- Trying-to-conceive mode with an estimated fertile window and an LH test plan.

### Privacy
- No account, no servers, no tracking. Cyclus has no internet access.
- Your data is encrypted on your device; optional app lock with biometrics or a Cyclus PIN.
- Privacy Center: see where your data is and erase everything at any time.

### Your data
- Encrypted backup file you can import again, also on a new phone.
- Encrypted device backup and restore.
- Doctor Visit Pack: a PDF summary for your appointment, in German or English, including your questions.
- Health Connect: import data and, if you like, write your entries.

### Look and feel
- Colour themes including Material You, dark theme, tablets and foldables.
- Widgets, a Quick Settings tile and discreet reminders (off until you turn them on).
- German and English.

Requires Android 10 or newer. Cyclus is not for birth control and is not a medical device; predictions are estimates.
