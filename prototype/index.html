Cyclus prototype

Clickable iPhone prototype for user interviews. Everything runs on sample data in this page. The period and fertility estimates come from the same models as the design spec, so logging changes them live.

Interview tasks
  1. Your period started today. Log it.
  2. When is your next period expected, and how sure is the app?
  3. Log cramps, a headache and how you slept.
  4. Trying scenario: log today's LH test as positive. What changed?
  5. Make a summary for your gynecologist without sexual activity in it.
  6. Stop period details showing on your Lock Screen.

Sample data only, dated around 22 Nov 2026. Nothing is stored or sent. Not a medical device. Fertility estimates are not for birth control.
